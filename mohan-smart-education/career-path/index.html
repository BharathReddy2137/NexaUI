CareerPath is a responsive career exploration workspace with a four-phase roadmap, editable milestone checkboxes, skill confidence controls, add/remove skill and project forms, and searchable career cards with interest filters. Career information and timelines are illustrative, not employment guarantees. Roadmap completion persists in localStorage.

Sample data is provided for the hackathon demo. Explore the interactive sections to see the workflow.
